THE STORY BEHIND TERACOPIA

I've spent more nights awake inside my dreams than most people spend awake in daylight.

Thousands of lucid dreams. Hundreds of out-of-body experiences. Not as a researcher studying the phenomenon from the outside — as someone who's lived inside it, night after night, for years.

ORIGIN

It started the way it does for a lot of people — a dream so vivid I woke up unsure which world was real. But instead of forgetting it by breakfast, I got curious. What was that moment right before I realized I was dreaming? Could I find it again on purpose?

I could. And once I knew that was possible, I couldn't leave it alone. I spent years testing everything — every induction method, every reality check, every theory about why some nights worked and others didn't — not to write a book about it eventually, but because I genuinely wanted to know what was on the other side of sleep.

Somewhere along the way, "occasionally" turned into "most nights." Lucid dreams turned into full out-of-body experiences. What began as curiosity became, without my really planning it, a practice.

I.

Thousands of nights, one practice

Not a technique I read once and tried a few times — a practice built and rebuilt across thousands of lucid dreams and hundreds of out-of-body experiences. I've made every mistake there is to make with this, which is exactly why the method holds up.

II.

The name arrived the way everything else here did

"Teracopia" came to me in a dream, fully formed. It felt right immediately — a made-up word for a very real place: the space just past the edge of waking life, where there's more room to explore than most people ever realize.

III.

Deciding to teach it

For a long time this was just mine. But the more I talked to people about it, the more obvious it became that the hard part isn't the dreaming — it's not knowing where to start, or trusting that it's learnable at all. I decided to put everything I'd taught myself into a real, structured method someone else could actually use.

IV.

What Teracopia is building toward

The guide and the ebook are the starting point, not the destination. Teracopia is becoming a full practice — writing, coaching, and eventually a community for people doing this together. The long-term vision is even bigger: a physical school one day, teaching metaphysics alongside practical self-sufficiency skills — the kind of place I wish had existed when I was starting out.

HOW I APPROACH THIS

What Teracopia believes

I

Direct experience over theory

Everything taught here has been lived first, not just researched. If it's in the guide, it's because it worked, repeatedly, for years.

II

Practice over hype

No shortcuts, no miracle techniques. Lucidity is a skill built through small, repeated habits — the same way any real skill is built.

III

Freedom over fear

The dream world can feel strange or even frightening at first. The goal here is always the same: to help you feel curious instead of afraid, and in control instead of along for the ride.
