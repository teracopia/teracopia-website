WHAT IS TERACOPIA?

Teracopia is lucid dreaming education built on personal experience rather than theory. Everything taught here stems from thousands of nights testing what actually works.

The name Teracopia came from a dream, a place of abundance for expanding your awareness and reconnecting with your higher self.

ORIGIN STORY

I found out about lucid dreaming when I was 13, and had one without even trying. I got hooked immediately, and started reading books on the topic, scouring the internet for information and personal stories, and developing my own practice for inducing lucid dreams on purpose.

It took a few weeks of testing reality checks, intention writing, dream journaling, and different induction methods before I had my next one, but once I figured out which parts of my routine actually made the difference, the frequency took off.

I quickly found there wasn't a ton of relevant information out there. Most of what existed was written by researchers back in the 80s and 90s, studies conducted without modern technology, and there wasn't a real face for lucid dreaming or out-of-body experience.

That felt like an opportunity: to share more up to date and digestible information. My goal with this guide is to bring together my knowledge from years of research alongside my own experience of lucid dreaming thousands of times over, for a version of my younger self who wished something like this existed.
