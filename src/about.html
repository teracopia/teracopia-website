MY STORY

It took me months
to have my first lucid dream.

I've had thousands of lucid dreams over the years, but I didn't start out that way.
I wrote this book for a version of my younger self, what I wish I had when I was first beginning on my journey.

WHAT IS TERACOPIA?

Teracopia is lucid dreaming education built on personal experience rather than theory. Everything taught here stems from thousands of nights testing what actually works.

The name Teracopia came from a dream, a place of abundance for expanding your awareness and reconnecting with your higher self.

ORIGIN STORY

I first found out about lucid dreaming when I was 13, and after months of trying, I had my first real experience.
I was immediately hooked and started consuming everything I could on the topic, reading books, research studies, and personal stories before developing my own lucid dreaming practice.

Most of the information available on lucid dreaming is overly clinical, and difficult to implement.
My goal in building Teracopia is to combine my knowledge from years of research alongside my own personal experience, all in a digestible and simple practice for anyone to start lucid dreaming.
