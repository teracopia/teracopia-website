Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Teracopia ("we," "us") collects when you visit teracopia.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We built this site to be simple, so we keep the data we collect simple too.

Information we collect

When you sign up for our free guide or newsletter, we collect the email address (and, where the form asks for one, the name) you provide. When you purchase the book, our payment processor collects the billing information needed to complete the transaction — we do not see or store your full card details ourselves. We also collect basic, aggregated site-usage data (like which pages are visited and roughly how many people visit) through privacy-focused analytics that does not use cookies or track you individually across other sites.

How we use it

  • To send you the free guide, the emails you signed up for, and occasional updates about Teracopia — you can unsubscribe from any email at any time using the link at the bottom of it.
  • To deliver the ebook you purchased and to send purchase confirmations or receipts.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how people use the site so we can improve it.
  • To respond if you email us directly.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your email address with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Third-party services we use

We rely on a small number of trusted providers to run this site:

  • Kit (formerly ConvertKit) stores your email address and manages the newsletter and any email sequences you're subscribed to.
  • Stripe, via Kit Commerce, will process payments once checkout for the book is live. Stripe handles card details directly under its own privacy and security practices — we never see your full card number.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and provides the aggregated, non-cookie-based analytics mentioned above.

Cookies

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Our analytics provider measures traffic in aggregate without setting cookies or building a profile of individual visitors.

Your rights and choices

You can unsubscribe from any email at any time. You can also email us at quintoncapretta@gmail.com to ask what information we have about you, to request a copy of it, or to request that we delete it, and we'll take care of it promptly.

Children's privacy

This site and its content are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Terms of Sale

These terms apply when you purchase the Teracopia ebook or any other digital product from this site.

Digital delivery

The book is a digital product (PDF) delivered instantly after purchase — typically by email and/or a download link provided at checkout. No physical product is shipped.

Pricing

Prices are listed in US dollars and are shown at checkout before you complete your purchase. We may change prices at any time; the price you paid at checkout is the price that applies to your purchase.

Payment

Once checkout is live, payments will be processed securely by Stripe through Kit Commerce. We do not store your payment card details on our own systems. Right now, signing up on the book page only reserves your spot on the notify list — you will not be charged until checkout opens and you complete a purchase.

Refunds

Because the book is delivered digitally and instantly and can be read in full right after purchase, all sales are final and we don't offer refunds. If something went wrong with your order — a failed download, a duplicate charge, that kind of thing — email us at quintoncapretta@gmail.com and we'll sort it out.

Use of the material

When you purchase the book, you're buying a personal-use license to read it yourself. Please don't redistribute, resell, or share copies with people who haven't purchased it — it's a small operation, and that support is what lets us keep making this work.

Not medical or professional advice

Teracopia's guides and book describe personal practices around lucid dreaming and related experiences. They are offered for educational and informational purposes and are not medical, psychological, or professional advice. If you have a medical or mental health condition, please talk to a qualified professional before starting any new sleep-related practice.

Contact
